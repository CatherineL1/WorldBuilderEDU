WorldBuilder EDU

Turn every lesson into a living, breathing universe. Build cities from knowledge, defend them with physics, and merge worlds with classmates.

Try the Prototype

City of Concepts

Each subject you master erects a new structure—Math towers rise from correct equations, literary bridges link story realms, and science labs expand as you unlock experiments. Every building is interactive proof of what you know and how creatively you use it.

Defend & Expand

AI-driven Chaos Events attack your city: riddles, wobbling bridges, historical anomalies. Solve them to keep your skyline shining, or risk cracked towers until you re-learn and repair.

Collaborative Knowledge Merging

Stand near a classmate—or invite them online—and watch your realms fuse into one. Complete co-op quests that demand both of your skill sets and leave a permanent golden monument visible to the entire universe.

Sensory Bridges

Feel success: rainbow waterfalls grow as you master algebra; forests bloom with every new vocabulary word. Light, sound, and motion celebrate learning in real time.

Open-Source Learning Mods

Drop in custom puzzles, art packs, or entirely new mechanics. The world evolves forever because anyone—student, teacher, or indie dev—can add bricks to the galaxy.
